How an order works

A made-to-measure order follows the same five steps every time, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Tell us what you wantCome to the shop with your fabric, or a photo of the style you like.
  2. We take your measurementsThis takes about ten minutes, and we write them in your own record.
  3. Pay a depositHalf the labour price secures your order and starts the cutting.
  4. Come for a fittingFor suits and ceremony outfits we check the fit before the final stitching.
  5. Collect and pay the balanceMost orders take five to seven working days. Rush orders in two to three days cost 25% extra.
