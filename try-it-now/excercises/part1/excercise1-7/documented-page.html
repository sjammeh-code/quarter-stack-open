Yakar Football School of Excellence

Yakar FSE is a community football school in the Gambia. This page explains who can join and how to apply, whether you want to play or to coach.

About the school

Yakar FSE gives young players a structured place to train and grow, and it works with the wider community through local football. In July and August 2026 the club took part in a ten-team community tournament, with its opening match at the Abuko Primary School field.

How to apply

Players and coaches apply separately. Choose the group that matches you and follow the steps in order.

If you are a player

Applications are made online, and a parent or guardian should help any player who is a child.

  1. Open the player application form.
  2. Fill in every question with your full name, age and playing position.
  3. Submit the form, then wait for the club to contact you.
