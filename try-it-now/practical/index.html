About us

Built by football people, for the community

Yakar Football School of Excellence is run by people who know the Gambian game from the inside, including players with first division experience. We believe talent is everywhere in the Gambia and what many young players lack is structure, coaching and a platform.

Our mission

To develop disciplined, skilled players and good people through quality football education.

Our vision

A Gambia where every talented young player has a clear pathway to grow.

Our values

Respect, hard work, teamwork and community.
